Every vaccine, prescription and vet visit, in one place you can trust.

Pet Health Memory turns the paperwork from your vet into a clear health history for your dog, cat or any other pet. Scan a certificate or report, check what was found, and it's saved — linked to the original document.

SCAN A VET DOCUMENT. WE DO THE TYPING.
• Scan with the camera, or import a PDF or photo
• The text is read on your device, not on a server
• Vaccines, medication, dosage, visit dates, weight and next due dates are found for you
• Nothing is saved until you check it. Unclear details are marked so you can compare them with the original

A HISTORY YOU CAN SHOW YOUR VET

• Every record links back to the document it came from
• Export a tidy PDF summary before an appointment or a trip
